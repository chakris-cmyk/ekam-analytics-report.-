How these numbers are worked out
  • Source: PostHog project "Default project" (Ekam app), queried on Nov 11, 2026. Internal and test accounts (cohort 427816) are left out. Dates are UTC; Nov 11 is a partial day.
  • Active users per day are distinct people with a session that day. Daily counts can't be added up into distinct people over a range, so the range tile shows the daily average.
  • Sign-in completion here is daily sign-ins divided by daily sign-in starts. The person-level figure for the month is 70.6%.
  • A session runs from its first to its last user event; background events such as notifications don't count.
  • Sections marked "Oct 12 – Nov 11" are fixed for the whole month in this preview. The live report in claude.ai recalculates every section for any dates and country.
